### 🧩 Key Insights

- The **banking sector** dominates Singapore’s market capitalization, led by **DBS, OCBC, and UOB**, which collectively make up over **40%** of the STI.  
- **Singtel** remains Singapore’s most valuable non-bank company, offering exposure to digital infrastructure and regional telecom growth.  
- **ST Engineering**, **SGX**, and **Keppel** provide stability through diversified industrial and infrastructure income.  
- **CapitaLand Investment** adds global real estate and fund management exposure to the top 10 list.
