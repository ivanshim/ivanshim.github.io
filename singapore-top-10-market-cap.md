### 🧩 Key Insights

- The **banking sector** dominates Singapore’s corporate landscape, with **DBS, OCBC, and UOB** collectively making up over 40% of total STI market capitalization.  
- **Singtel** remains the most valuable non-bank company, providing stable dividends and regional digital infrastructure exposure.  
- **ST Engineering**, **SGX**, and **Keppel** deliver stability through diversified industrial and infrastructure income streams.  
- **CapitaLand Investment** offers global real-estate and fund-management exposure, rounding out the top 10.
