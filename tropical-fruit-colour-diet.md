# 🌴 Tropical Fruit Colour Diet (Monday–Friday)

A vibrant, nutrient-rich weekly plan featuring tropical fruits organized by colour — designed to maximize variety, flavour, and health benefits.  
Each day’s colour represents a unique combination of phytonutrients, antioxidants, and vitamins found in tropical fruits.

---

## 🍎 Monday – Red Fruits (Antioxidants & Heart Health)
| Fruits | Benefits |
|---------|-----------|
| **Watermelon 🍉** | Hydrating and rich in lycopene for heart health |
| **Red dragon fruit (pitaya)** | High in fiber and magnesium for gut and muscle support |
| **Pink guava** | Excellent vitamin C source and immune booster |

**Key Nutrients:** Lycopene, anthocyanins, vitamin C  
**Focus:** Cardiovascular protection and immune defense

---

## 🍊 Tuesday – Orange Fruits (Skin & Immunity)
| Fruits | Benefits |
|---------|-----------|
| **Papaya** | Aids digestion and provides beta-carotene for skin repair |
| **Ripe mango** | Rich in vitamin A and antioxidants for eye and skin health |
| **Persimmon (tropical variety)** | High in fiber and carotenoids, supports skin elasticity |

**Key Nutrients:** Beta-carotene, vitamin A  
**Focus:** Skin rejuvenation and immune support

---

## 🍋 Wednesday – Yellow Fruits (Energy & Digestion)
| Fruits | Benefits |
|---------|-----------|
| **Pineapple** | Contains bromelain for digestion and anti-inflammation |
| **Banana** | Source of potassium and natural energy |
| **Yellow passionfruit** | Provides vitamin C and fiber for gut health |

**Key Nutrients:** Potassium, bromelain, serotonin precursors  
**Focus:** Energy, gut comfort, and balanced mood

---

## 🥑 Thursday – Green Fruits (Detox & Vitality)
| Fruits | Benefits |
|---------|-----------|
| **Green guava** | Rich in fiber and vitamin C, aids detoxification |
| **Avocado** | High in healthy fats, potassium, and magnesium |
| **Starfruit (green, slightly underripe)** | Refreshing and mildly cleansing for liver and kidneys |

**Key Nutrients:** Chlorophyll, folate, magnesium  
**Focus:** Detoxification, vitality, and pH balance

---

## 🫐 Friday – Purple/Blue Fruits (Brain & Circulation)
| Fruits | Benefits |
|---------|-----------|
| **Mangosteen** | “Queen of fruits” — contains xanthones, strong antioxidants |
| **Java plum (jamun)** | Supports blood sugar balance and vascular health |
| **Purple dragon fruit** | High in fiber and anthocyanins, boosts brain function |

**Key Nutrients:** Anthocyanins, polyphenols  
**Focus:** Brain function, circulation, and anti-aging

### 🌈 Notes
- All fruits listed are *strictly tropical*, suited for equatorial climates.  
- Can be eaten fresh, blended, or in smoothie bowls.  
- For a weekend extension:  
  - **Saturday (White):** Coconut, lychee, longan  
  - **Sunday (Rainbow):** Mix of all colours for full-spectrum nutrition.
